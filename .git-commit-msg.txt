fix(merge): append instead of re-chunking, skip identical sessions, persist reset (0.1.0-rc.46)

/keeper import failed in the field with BYTE_GAP on the session that is still being
written: the merge rebuilt the session's descriptor list from byte zero, and the reader
then saw two different descriptor sets for the same session. The rollback restored the
previous vault, so nothing was lost.

Identical sessions are now skipped (no chunks, no manifest entry), a session that grew
receives only the bytes it lacks from the end of its current lineage, and reset is
persisted by writeManifest and honoured by the reader instead of being dropped silently.

Measured on the two real vaults: both verify ok with the same storeId and 11 sessions;
ten are byte-identical and only the growing session differs (PC 10,946,721 B vs pendrive
9,640,095 B). The stick had nothing the PC lacked, so the correct outcome is "nothing
new".
